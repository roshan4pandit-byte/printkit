Nozzle Guide

  • 0.2 mm — Fine details
  • 0.4 mm — All-round printing
  • 0.6 mm — Faster functional prints
  • 0.8 mm — Large fast prints

Layer Height

  • 0.08–0.12 mm — High detail
  • 0.16–0.20 mm — Normal quality
  • 0.24–0.28 mm — Faster printing
  • 0.30+ mm — Speed focused

Print Checklist

  • Check bed level
  • Check first layer
  • Use correct temperature
  • Dry wet filament
  • Check supports
  • Choose correct orientation
